# Leaflet for Example medicine A

This page is for patients who take Example medicine A.

Every pack of Example medicine A comes with a leaflet. It tells you how to take your medicine, what side effects it can cause and how to store it. You can also read the leaflet online.

- [Example medicine A patient leaflet (PDF only, 240KB)](/files/example-medicine-a-leaflet-2021.pdf)

You can ask for the leaflet in large print, braille or audio. Call our nurses on 0808 157 0123.

## Reporting side effects

<!-- protected -->
If you get any side effects, talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse. This includes any side effects not listed in the leaflet that comes with your medicine. You can also report side effects directly through the [Yellow Card scheme](https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk). Reporting side effects helps provide more information about this medicine.
