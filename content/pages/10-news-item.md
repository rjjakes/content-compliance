# Patient open day, November 2024

This page is for patients.

Join us for our patient open day on Saturday 16 November 2024. The day is for anyone who lives with hay fever or eczema, and for their families and carers.

Our nurses will be on hand to answer your questions. There will also be short talks on managing your symptoms through the year, and a chance to meet other people with the same condition.

![](images/open-day-2024.jpg)

The open day runs from 10am to 3pm. Tea, coffee and a light lunch will be provided. Places are limited, so book early by calling 0808 157 0456.

If you cannot come on the day, our nurses are happy to talk to you by phone. You can also ask for a copy of the talks to be sent to you by email after the event.
