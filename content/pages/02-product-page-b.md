# Example medicine B

This page is for patients who have been prescribed Example medicine B.

Example medicine B is a cream for dry, itchy skin caused by eczema. It is clinically proven to soothe itching and help your skin feel more comfortable. The cream has no added perfume and can be used every day.

## How to use the cream

<!-- protected -->
Use a thin layer on the affected skin twice a day. Do not use it on broken or weeping skin. Wash your hands after use.
<!-- /protected -->

Smooth the cream gently into your skin until it has gone in. It can be used with your usual moisturiser. Leave a gap of about 30 minutes between the two.

## Who can use it

The cream can be used by adults and children aged 2 years and over. Talk to your doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you have used a steroid cream in the last month.

## Possible side effects

Some people get a mild stinging or burning feeling when they first use the cream. This usually stops after a few days. If your skin gets more red or sore, stop using the cream and talk to your doctor.

## Reporting side effects

<!-- protected -->
If you get any side effects, talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse. This includes any side effects not listed in the leaflet that comes with your medicine. You can also report side effects directly through the [Yellow Card scheme](https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk). Reporting side effects helps provide more information about this medicine.
