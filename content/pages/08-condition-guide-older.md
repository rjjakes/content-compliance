# A guide to hay fever

This page is for patients.

## What is hay fever?

Hay fever is a common allergy. It is caused by pollen from grass, trees and weeds. It is most often seen in the spring and summer months, when the pollen count is high. Many people find that it gets better as they get older, but for others it can last for many years.

## Signs to look out for

The main signs of hay fever are sneezing, a runny nose and itchy eyes. Some people also get a blocked nose, a cough or a headache. They may also feel tired, because their sleep is affected. These signs can be mistaken for a cold, but a cold does not usually last as long.

## Ways to help yourself

There are a number of things that can be done to reduce the amount of pollen you come into contact with. Windows should be kept shut on days when the pollen count is high. Clothes should be changed and washed after time outdoors. Sunglasses can be worn to help keep pollen out of the eyes. Pets should be wiped down with a damp cloth when they come inside, as pollen can be carried in their fur.

## Treatments

Treatments for hay fever can be bought from a pharmacy. These include tablets, nose sprays and eye drops. Your pharmacist will be able to advise you on which treatment is right for you. If your symptoms are not helped by these treatments, an appointment should be made with your GP.
