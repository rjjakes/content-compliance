# Example medicine C

This page is for patients who have been prescribed Example medicine C.

## What it is for

Example medicine C is a tablet used to treat migraine attacks. It helps ease the headache and other symptoms, like feeling sick or being sensitive to light. It does not stop migraines from starting, so it should only be taken once an attack has begun.

## How to take it

<!-- protected -->
Take one tablet as soon as a migraine starts. If the migraine comes back, you can take a second tablet at least 2 hours after the first. Do not take more than 2 tablets in 24 hours.
<!-- /protected -->

Swallow the tablet whole with water. It can be taken with or without food. If the first tablet does not help at all, do not take a second one for the same attack. Talk to your doctor instead.

## Possible side effects

Like all medicines, Example medicine C can cause side effects, although not everybody gets them. Common side effects include:

- feeling dizzy
- feeling tired
- a warm or tingling feeling in your skin
- feeling sick

These usually pass within an hour or two.

Call 999 straight away if you have chest pain or tightness, or if your face, lips or throat start to swell.

## Who cannot take it

Example medicine C is not suitable for everyone. Tell your doctor before you start if you:

- have heart problems or high blood pressure
- have had a stroke
- are pregnant or breastfeeding
- take other medicines for migraine

## Storing your tablets

Keep the tablets in the blister pack until you need one. Store them below 25°C and out of the sight and reach of children.
