# Support while you take Example medicine A

This page is for patients who take Example medicine A.

## What the support programme offers

A support programme is offered to everyone who takes Example medicine A. It is run by a team of nurses and can be joined at any time. Support is given by phone, email or text, as suits you. Reminders can be sent to help you take your medicine on time and order more when you need it. Questions can be answered by a nurse, but advice about your own care should always come from your doctor.

![](images/support-programme-steps.png)

## How to join

You can join by calling the nurse helpline on 0808 157 0123, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. You will be asked for your name, date of birth and the name of your doctor. Your details are kept private and are only used to run the programme. You can leave the programme at any time.

## Keeping track of your treatment

You should keep a list of all the medicines you take. This includes any drugs you buy yourself, such as painkillers or vitamins. The list should be brought each time you see your doctor or nurse. Any changes you notice should be written down, so they can be talked about at your next visit. If a dose is missed, the leaflet in the box should be checked to find out what to do.

## Travelling with your medicine

Your medicine should be kept in its original box when you travel. It should be carried in your hand luggage, not in a suitcase that goes in the hold. A letter from your doctor may be needed if you are going abroad. Extra supplies should be ordered well before you go.

## Side effects

<!-- protected -->
If you get any side effects, talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse. This includes any side effects not listed in the leaflet that comes with your medicine. You can also report side effects directly through the [Yellow Card scheme](https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk). Reporting side effects helps provide more information about this medicine.
